<h1 align="center">train_waybills</h1>
<h3 align="center">Service simulating the ordering and transport of freight wagons by rail between different companies.</h3>
<p>It consists of three areas:
<ol>
  <li>Public area
    <div>You can register a company or log in to the service.</div>
  </li>
  <li>Administrative area
    <div>You can add, remove, or modify: railway wagons, orders, companies, employees, and loading points.<br>It has the ability to display detailed information about an order.</div>
  </li>
  <li>Company area
    <div>You can add, remove, or modify orders.</div>
  </li>
  <li>Employee area
    <div>Contains a wagon search engine where, after entering the wagon number, information about the wagon status or destination point is displayed. Changing the wagon status requires confirmation, e.g., by loading the wagon.</div>
  </li>
</ol>
</p>
